# Draft Reply: Rainwater Storage & Microplastics

**In reply to:** Question about plastic rainwater storage units, microplastics concerns, and permaculture community sentiment.

---

**Draft:**

Good question! Yeah, most of what you see shared are standard HDPE (food-grade polyethylene) tanks — same material as milk jugs, rated UV-resistant and considered safe for potable water storage. The permaculture community talks about microplastics for sure, but the general sentiment is pragmatic: HDPE doesn't leach nearly as much as PVC or polycarbonate, and the alternative — letting rainwater run off into storm drains — is a bigger loss. Some folks go with ferrocement or steel cisterns for long-term setups, but for most backyard systems the food-grade poly tanks are the accepted tradeoff. Always good to ask though.
