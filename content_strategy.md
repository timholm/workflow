# holm.community Content Strategy

## What Is holm.community?
A Mastodon instance documenting real life in San Diego's South Bay — through the eyes of someone actually living it. Not curated influencer content. Real people, real groups, real moments.

## Voice
- First person, present tense — "Morning run with Chula Vista Run Club" not "Had a great time at..."
- Short captions. Let the photo do the work.
- Tag the groups and people when possible — builds connections.
- No hashtag spam. 2-3 relevant tags max per post.
- Authentic > polished. A blurry action shot of a drum circle beats a perfect sunset.

## Content Pillars (What You Post)

### 1. Community Groups (60% of posts)
The core. You're embedded in real SD groups — this is what makes holm.community different.
- Group run photos (Chula Vista Run Club, Milestone Running)
- Cycling ride shots (Rouleur Thursday Night, SDBC Saturday)
- Hiking group summits (SD Day Hikers, Take A Hike! SD)
- Beach cleanup crews (Surfrider, I Love A Clean SD)
- Paddling groups (SD SUP/Kayak Meetup, Aqua Adventures)
- Photo walks (Darkroomers, SD Photography Collective)

**What to capture:** People in motion. Group energy. The before and after — staging area chatter, post-run beers, cleanup tally boards.

### 2. Local Culture (25% of posts)
The texture of South Bay life.
- Farmers market vendors (Little Italy Mercato, Hillcrest)
- Barrio Logan murals and art walks
- Pepper Park lowrider shows
- Street food and taco spots
- Third Ave Chula Vista night life
- Local music, drum circles at IB

**What to capture:** Hands making food. Paint on walls. Chrome reflecting sunset. The details, not the wide shot.

### 3. The Land (15% of posts)
San Diego's nature — but always tied to an experience, not just a landscape.
- Sunrise from a run, not just a sunrise
- Trail view from a hike with the group, not just a trail view
- Sunset from a beach cleanup, not just a sunset
- Birds from a SD Bird Alliance walk, not just birds

**What to capture:** The context. Show the group in the landscape. Show your shoes on the trail. Make it personal.

## Post Types

| Type | Frequency | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **Photo + short caption** | Daily (auto-scheduled) | "6:29am. Balboa Park. November Project doesn't care if you're fast. #NPSD #SanDiego" |
| **Photo carousel / thread** | 2-3x per week | "Saturday with SDBC — 40 miles from Balboa Park to Torrey Pines and back. [thread of 4-5 photos]" |
| **Boost / engage** | Daily (AI handles) | Boost posts from groups you're part of, reply to followers |
| **Weekly recap** | Sunday (AI auto-compiles) | "This week: 3 runs, 1 ride, 1 beach cleanup, 47 miles, 12 new people met." |

## Posting Schedule
- **Weekday posts:** 1 per day, auto-scheduled from previous evening's photo session
- **Weekend posts:** 2-3 per day, posted in real-time from events + AI schedules extras
- **Best posting times for Mastodon:** 7-9am, 12-1pm, 5-7pm (AI handles timing)

## Content Rules
1. Never post someone's face without asking. Groups are fine — individuals, ask first.
2. Always tag/mention the group or org when posting about their event.
3. Don't sell anything. This is community, not marketing.
4. Boost others more than you post. 2:1 ratio minimum.
5. Respond to every reply within 24 hours (AI drafts, you approve weekly if needed).

## Growth Strategy
- **Month 1-3:** Show up consistently. Post daily. Become a regular in 2-3 groups.
- **Month 4-6:** People start recognizing you. Tag you in their posts. Cross-pollination begins.
- **Month 7-12:** holm.community becomes the place people check to see what's happening in SD's outdoor/community scene.
- **Year 2+:** Other people want accounts on holm.community. You open registration. Community grows itself.

## Metrics That Matter
- Followers: vanity, but track it
- Boosts: this means your content resonates enough to share
- Replies: this means people feel connected enough to talk to you
- New group members who found you on Mastodon: the real metric
